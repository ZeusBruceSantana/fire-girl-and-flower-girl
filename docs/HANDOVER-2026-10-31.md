# Handover brief: the improvement program (from the review panel)

**For:** the next Claude Code session(s) in this repo · **From:** the session that built epics 0 to 10
**Date:** 2026-10-31 · **Owner's bar:** professional and slick, not childish; fleshed out; deep story lines
the owner wants to spend real time on.

Read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/STATUS.md` first. This brief distills eight reports in `docs/review/`
(five reviewers who played the game headlessly, three researchers). The full reports are the source of
truth for details, numbers and citations; this is the plan.

---

## 0. Where the game stands

Epics 0 to 10 are built (88 tests, all green): a school day loop with four classes, lunch and
friendship, an overworld of five maps, turn-based battles powered by reading, shops, bag and gear,
crafting, the Lair, town cases, myths storybook, art easel, sound and music, Electric Girl's story,
and a Cloudflare relay for separate devices (not yet deployed). All art on screen is vector
placeholder; the owner is generating painted art from `docs/ART-PROMPTS.md`.

The panel's one-line verdict: the bones are right (the direction, the systems, the architecture), but
it plays like a quiz with a nicer frame, has no visual system, no story spine, and several day-one
bugs. Fix in this order.

---

## Phase A. Day-one blockers (do first, one session, all S/M)

From `05-robustness.md` and `01-first-time-experience.md`. Each has repro steps there.

1. **Battle softlock:** the word card (`#challenge`) renders under `#battle` (DOM order, no z-index).
   The first battle on a real tablet cannot be finished. Fix the layer order with explicit z-indexes:
   dialogue < menu < battle < challenge < parent. (S)
2. **Dialogue supersede freezes the day:** `Dialogue.show` calls `close(true)` which never resolves the
   previous promise; `Day.wake/runClass/bedtime` hold `Day.running` with no try/finally; `onArrive`
   fires while a dialogue is open. Resolve superseded dialogues (with null), wrap every `running` block
   in try/finally, and gate `onArrive`/`MapStage.arrive` behind `sceneLayer`. (S)
3. **Offline is broken:** `sw.js` caches only files linked from `index.html`; art folders are never
   pre-cached. Generate a tested manifest of every file under `art/`, `data/`, `js/` and pre-cache it;
   shorten the network-first timeout; add a test that the manifest covers every file on disk. (M)
4. **One global `sceneLayer` is a hole:** the HUD portrait opens the menu over a class dialogue;
   closing one overlay nulls the layer while another is open. Make it a stack. Block the HUD behind
   dialogues and challenges. (S)
5. **Lunch can strand the day:** lunch ends only when every present player talks kindly or buys ice
   cream; "No" to "Do you ever miss home?" counts as unkind. Lunch ends when any player has done one
   thing or the bell rings; honest answers are never "unkind". (S)
6. **Challenge cards accept the other twin's finger** and drop her sister's word to box 0. Route taps by
   seat (left half / right half) on class cards. (S)
7. **Tap targets:** enforce floors from `--btn` on choices, tabs, Bye, tiles, tappable words (currently
   ×.6/.7 by design and tiny on phones). (S)
8. **Save growth:** prune `log.seen` and `log.tail` (never store painting data URLs in the tail), warn
   the parent screen when `saveNow` fails. (M)
9. **Dead ends on day one:** wardrobe, chalkboard out of hours, fountain, every Lair station say "not
   yet". Give each a real small interaction or hide it until it works. (S)
10. **One-tap battles and chain battles:** level-1 Grumpy Weed dies to one Spark; after a win the next
    trouble walks into the standing party. Trouble invulnerability for 2 s after a battle, troubles
    back off, and the balance fixes in Phase B. (S)

Also: restore place on reload (every reload starts at the gate), name length caps, word-list hygiene
(spaces, duplicates), speech interrupting itself, canvas performance (cache the vignette, limit
shadowBlur).

## Phase B. Systems rebalance (one session; numbers from `03-systems-and-balance.md`)

1. **Pay ember before the word, and stop refunding it in battle.** Today a move costs 1 to 5 and the
   word that powers it earns 3 to 4, so the meter sits at 10 and nothing is a choice. Class and chests
   earn ember; battles spend it. Level-1 move stays free. (M)
2. **Fix the combo so it is cross-sister** (`lastMatched` tracks the previous hero's element, not the
   same hero's), and give each princess one off-element move at level 2 so matching is a decision. (M)
3. **Together spells:** cost 4 each, drop the extra ×2; they should be a finisher, not every turn. (S)
4. **XP:** halve battle xp and scale by level difference; current ladder is done by day 4. Target
   level 3 on day 2, level 5 on day 7, level 7 on day 14 with classes plus one adventure a day. (S)
5. **Tangle cooldown** (never two turns in a row for the same girl); boss phase-2 gets a new action. (S)
6. **Party roles:** one support move per princess at level 2 (Fire: Warm = next sister's move ×1.5;
   Flower: Dew = clear soot/tangle; Electric: Hum = +2 ember to a sister). (M)
7. **Hearts:** ladder 3/6/10/15, two talks a day, fund share 1.0, and fix the Lair fund losing half of
   every hearts *spend* (apply fundShare only to positive amounts). (S)
8. **Coins:** class coins 2/4/6 by stars; sinks that matter (treats, cosmetic Lair bits, badge polish);
   the shops should not be bought out by day 9. (S)
9. **Second-week content from data:** two-trouble encounters, night-only and weather-only troubles,
   a daily wanted board at Pebble's, trouble levels scaled to party level. (M)
10. **A visible long game:** level cap 10 with unlocks at 8 to 10, befriended troubles (a Medallium
    page), sticker/collection goals, and the badge wall as the spine (Phase D). (L)

Unused today and should either work or go: `stats.brave/kind/clever`, `QUESTS`, `AREAS`, the
`telescope` upgrade, the `three-stars` badge awarded forever.

## Phase C. Interface system (one to two sessions; `02-visual-polish.md`)

The verdict: good bones, no system. 15 font sizes, 12 radii, four button styles, three Close controls,
placeholder diamonds for every item, "?" walls on five of seven menu tabs, overlapping name tags,
the class target range drawn under the HUD.

1. **One type system:** bundle one rounded display face (OFL, offline) for titles, names and buttons,
   system sans for body, a 6-step scale as CSS tokens; stop weight-900 body text. (M)
2. **One button family:** primary (gold), confirm (green, only "I am done"), quiet (parchment outline);
   same radius, shadow and press state everywhere. (S)
3. **HUD hierarchy and collisions:** objective at body size on one line with an icon; framed counters;
   drop party name tags on maps; one place-banner style that never overlaps the HUD. (S)
4. **Real empty states:** silhouettes not diamonds; a drawn map with fog instead of a grid; a badge wall
   with dim slots; Journal never prints raw flag ids. (M)
5. **Party on screen in battle**, a unified battle HUD, a glyph per move. (M)
6. **Challenge card layout rules** (never shrink below legible; two cards max side by side; motion on
   enter and on a wrong answer). (S)
7. **Target range visible** under the HUD; it is the "class as play" element. (S)
8. **Category silhouette icons** for every item (wand, crown, cape, boots, gem, seed, book, paint). (S)
9. **Lair upgrades as a room**, not a pricing list. (L)
10. **Frame-sheet hook** (`border-image`) so the owner's painted frames drop in. (M)

Ceremonies (from `01`): class end, battle win, level-up and bedtime are single text boxes today. Each
needs a short sequence: the stars appear one by one, the trouble skips off, a level-up card, a bedtime
page with the day's picture. Motion principles and `prefers-reduced-motion` are in `02`.

## Phase D. Learning as the move (one to two sessions; `08-research-learning-mechanics.md`)

The research is blunt: learning sticks when the learning act *is* the game move (Habgood and
Ainsworth, 7× longer voluntary play). "Pick a spell, then a challenge pops up" is still a toll booth.

1. **The word is the spell card.** In battle the move buttons are the words themselves (three cards,
   one is the spell's word, read aloud when tapped after the first try); no separate challenge screen.
   The hint ladder lives on the cards. (M)
2. **Many one-word moments across the day** instead of one five-word class block: signs, doors,
   chests, NPC lines with tappable choices, the shop till. Classes become shorter and denser. (M)
3. **A phonics pattern layer** (`data/phonics.js`) under the parent's list so Leitner spaces patterns,
   not only words; UFLI order; cumulative review. (M)
4. **Sound boxes** replace free letter slots in Build-the-word; tiles speak their sound. (S)
5. **Heart words** marked (only the irregular part by memory). (S)
6. **Stuck detection** (8 s idle or second miss) before any hint; the helper asks before telling. (S)
7. **Learned = three clean retrievals** with expanding gaps and a next-day return; promotion to box 3
   needs a recall (build) success, not recognition. (S)
8. **Three-tier help per pattern**, never announced. (M)
9. **Numbers as play:** coin stacks whose height is value, slice to make change, the girls run their
   own till in the Lair. (M)
10. **Parent page:** Confident / Getting there / Tricky by pattern and word, a weekly first-try line,
    minutes separate from words learned, one bedtime tip. No timers, no words-per-minute. (M)

Six new verbs are sketched in `08` section 5: pick the lock, cast by the tile, brew the potion, read
the sign to find the secret, run the till, teach your sparkle. Build the first three in this phase.

## Phase E. Story: Season One (the owner's priority; several sessions, with the owner)

Two reports converged (`04-story-and-writing.md`, `07-research-rpg-craft.md`): there is no spine,
no stakes, no antagonist; Fire Girl and Flower Girl have zero lines; only E speaks. Both propose a
hidden-history mystery around the Ember Bloom spell with the Headmistress at its heart, a kind
antagonist pressure, ten chapters in three acts tied to places and classes, and a badge wall as the
visible spine. Merge the two into one bible with the owner, then write.

**Working proposal (merge of both):**
- **The mystery:** a hundred years ago three princesses (fire, flower, lightning) tried the Ember Bloom
  spell at Festival Hill; it went wrong (an enormous harmless firework that scared both kingdoms) and
  they were told never to work together again. The Headmistress was one of them. The spark that jumped
  the river never went out: it is a lonely child-flame, the **Wisp**, hiding in the Torch Cave and
  causing the town's small troubles. The Headmistress has been secretly growing ember-flowers there,
  hoping someone braver would try again. Ember-flower petals turn up around town from chapter 1.
- **Kind pressure:** **Inspector Quill** from Capital will move the Festival to the city unless the
  "unexplained incidents" stop: a clock, never cruel.
- **Three acts, ten chapters** (full table in `07` section 3): Act 1 *Junior Helpers* (the bottom
  shelf, Pebble's hat, the gate that is two seasons, who took the lamps); Act 2 *The Three That Don't
  Mix* (the pond that won't thaw, the Torch Cave turning point, the girl from the clouds); Act 3 *The
  Festival* (Pure of Heart as a no-battle chapter, the rehearsal tour, Ember Bloom). Each chapter: one
  place, one class that powers it, one Helper Badge, one battle lesson, one clue on the case board, a
  reveal and a choice. Secondary arcs end in-season (Sneezy Smoke's search for warmth, the Moss
  princess's rivalry, the Ice princess's lantern, Pebble getting her police back). Year 2 seed:
  Stormhaven.
- **Characters:** want / flaw / turn for each of the three girls (write their lines first; they are
  mute today), the Headmistress (the ally who knew), Hoot (misremembers on purpose), Pebble, Quill,
  Mrs. Crumb, all six friends (restore the bible traits: Wren secretly loves warm things; Gale is the
  rumor). One voice tag per character; the Moss princess as the kind rival.
- **Reactive lines** (Hades): a pool keyed on state (day, badges, last battle, weather, who is here)
  so people acknowledge the loop; friends initiate through notes and lost items.
- **Style guide** (`04` section 4): 3 to 7 words for decoded text, up to 12 read aloud; no contractions
  for decoded text, the Headmistress, Abacus and Quill; humor that lands at 7 and for the parent
  (literal-minded troubles, hopeless adults, callbacks); retire "super effective"; vary the
  "Hmm. Think again." and reward lines (five variants each).
- **Fix now (S):** the `cold-snap` quest spoils Ember Bloom; "two kingdoms" at the festival; the
  `(unused)` adventure beat; GAME-DESIGN section 9 culprits differ from `cases.js` (data wins).

**Method:** write `docs/STORY-BIBLE.md` (mystery, characters, chapter outlines, style guide) and get the
owner's edits before writing lines. Then `data/story.js` becomes the chapter engine (beats with
conditions, choices, flags), and every class, case and adventure gets its chapter hooks. The twins'
lines go in first.

## Phase F. Depth and long tail (after A to E)

From `06-research-kids-rpgs.md` and `07`: elemental strong/weak tags on move cards after first meeting
a trouble; an optional stylish-tap flourish with a wide window and no penalty; an onlooker audience in
battles; friendship ranks that unlock battle assists; a Medallium page for befriended troubles; badges
with mechanical effects and a point budget; paintings placed into the world by NPC request; pick
today's goal at the dorm; routes as choice nodes; the "hum/wave" no-score verb; collectibles with a lore
card and a proximity chime; finale plus "Year 2" as a kid New Game+. What not to copy: member perks,
luck-based befriending, daily lockouts, text-only dialogue.

## Working method

- One phase at a time; lanes for independent files (pre-wire `index.html` and `tests/index.html`
  stubs first, as before). Every lane owns its files and its test file; the full suite must stay green.
- Keep `docs/STATUS.md` current and copy it to Drive "Reading RPG Handoff / from-code" after each phase.
- Never polish placeholders instead of fixing structure; the owner's paintings are coming.
- Plain language in commits and docs; no family details in the repo.
