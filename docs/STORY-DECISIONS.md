## A. The spine

1. **The hundred-year-old wound as the mystery.** Three princesses (fire, flower, lightning) tried Ember Bloom
   at Festival Hill a hundred years ago; it went wrong (an enormous harmless firework that scared both kingdoms)
   and they were told never to work together again. The girls uncover it piece by piece. Recommend yes.
   Alternative: a different mystery (a lost object, a missing person, a curse).
2. **The Headmistress was one of the three.** She grows ember-flowers in the Torch Cave in secret, hoping someone
   braver tries again. The ally who knew, never a villain. Recommend yes. Alternative: she stays simply kind and worried.
3. **The Wisp.** The spark that jumped the river never went out: a lonely child-flame in the Torch Cave causing the
   town's small troubles by accident; befriended, not defeated. Recommend yes. Alternative: no creature.
4. **The clock.** Inspector Quill from Capital will move the Festival to the city unless the incidents stop. Kind,
   never cruel, a bureaucrat with a ledger. Alternatives: a rival school, a grumpy mayor, or only the festival date.
5. **The ending.** The three cast Ember Bloom on Festival Hill with the Headmistress watching and both kingdoms see it
   work. Bigger (the kingdoms reconcile), quieter (the girls and the Headmistress), or open (a Year 2 hook)?
