# The world of Ember Bloom

The story bible, version 2. Everything here is also in `data/world.js` and its friends, which is
what the game actually reads. Change the words there; this page is the version to read aloud at
bedtime.

## The three kingdoms

**Emberfall** is a kingdom of warm stone, lanterns and bakeries. It is always a little too hot.
Its people are quick and loud and kind. Its princess is **Fire Girl**. Her magic is light and
heat: she can light a candle from across the room, warm a cold friend, and make a dark room glow.

**Petalvale** is a kingdom of meadows, orchards and bees. It is always a little too damp. Its
people are patient and clever and kind. Its princess is **Flower Girl**. Her magic is growing and
calming: she can make a seed sprout, call up a vine, and fill a square with flowers.

**Stormhaven** is a kingdom in the clouds, higher than the highest mountain, where the houses
have lightning rods for chimneys and the streets hum. It is always a little too loud. Its people
are curious and fast and kind. Its princess is **Electric Girl**, who everyone calls **E**. Her
magic is sparks and storms: she can zap a lamp alive, hum a tune into a bell, flash a whole
hallway bright, and, when she is very excited, call a small storm. She finds it very hard to hold
still.

## Why they kept apart

Fire burns flowers. A hundred years ago, a spark from an Emberfall festival jumped the river and
burned a Petalvale orchard. Nobody was hurt, but the two kingdoms decided it was safer to keep to
their own sides of the river. Children of Emberfall were told, "Never go near the flower people,
you will hurt them." Children of Petalvale were told, "Never go near the fire people, they will
hurt you."

Stormhaven kept apart from both, because a kingdom in the clouds is hard to get to, and because
every time a Stormhaven visitor came down to say hello, something caught fire or a garden got
rained on. Children of Stormhaven were told, "Keep your sparks to yourself. You are too much for
the ground."

All three were a little bit right, and all three had forgotten something.

## What they forgot

Petals hold cool dew. A vine can wrap a flame until it falls asleep. Flower Girl's gentlest
spell, **Dew** (she calls it Bloom Breath), can put out Fire Girl's fire. So fire is not simply
stronger. Neither of them can win, which is a good reason to stop trying.

And lightning is both. A spark from the sky can start a fire, which Fire Girl thinks is wonderful.
A storm brings rain, which Flower Girl thinks is wonderful. But lightning is wild and never stays
put, and only two things can calm it: warm dry air, which is Fire Girl's, and deep roots in the
ground, which are Flower Girl's. E needs them both to feel steady. Each of the three needs the
other two.

And there is something better still. Fire warms a seed so it sprouts early. Ash makes the soil
rich. A flower can cup a flame and hold it steady, a lantern that never burns. A storm over a warm
garden makes things grow in winter. A spark in a flame makes a firework. And when all three kinds
of magic work together, it is called **Ember-Bloom magic**, and nobody has seen it in a hundred
years.

## The Academy

**Thistledown Royal Academy of Small Magic** sits exactly on the river, half in Emberfall, half
in Petalvale, with a tall tower that pokes into the clouds so that Stormhaven can send a princess
down a rope ladder. Every princess with a little magic goes there, and they live there, in a long
dormitory with a window for each bed. It is a very small school. Most years have only a few
students. The girls' year has exactly three.

The Academy has one rule that matters: **princesses do not go past the garden wall.** The Woods
are past the wall. The town is past the Woods. The Headmistress says the rule is for their safety.
The girls think the rule is for the Headmistress's nerves.

The Academy has another rule, older than the first: **nobody is ever sent away.** When a princess
breaks a rule, she does not get expelled. She takes the **Pure of Heart test**. The Headmistress
sits her down with a cup of cocoa and tells her a story about someone who needs help, and asks
what she would do. There is no trick to it. A kind answer passes. An unkind answer gets, "Hmm. Let
us think about that again," and the story is told once more. Everyone passes in the end, because
everyone, given enough cocoa, is kind.

## How E got in

Stormhaven had never sent a princess to the Academy. The Headmistress worried about the curtains.
So E took the Pure of Heart test at the top of the tower, before she was allowed down the ladder.
The story was about a lamp that had gone out in a stranger's window. E said she would light it,
and then she would stay to make sure it did not go out again, and then she would ask if the
stranger wanted a second lamp. The Headmistress said that was three kind answers, which was more
than anyone had given before, and let her down the ladder. E only set one curtain slightly on
fire on the way.

## The first day

Seats at the Academy are chosen by a hat that pulls names out of a jar, and the hat has never once
been argued with. The hat sat Fire Girl and Flower Girl at the same desk, and E at the desk
behind.

On the first day, Fire Girl sneezed and set the desk on fire. Everyone ran. Flower Girl did not.
She blew gently, and her petals put the fire out. The desk was fine. Fire Girl said sorry. Flower
Girl said, "It was a very good sneeze." E said, "Do it again!"

They have been best friends ever since.

## The tunnel and the lair

It was Flower Girl's idea, which surprised everyone. Behind the bottom shelf of the library, where
the dullest books live, there is a loose stone. Flower Girl's vines pulled the stones out one by
one. Fire Girl dried the mud and warmed the air so it was never cold. Later, when E came, her
sparks lit a row of tiny lamps all the way down.

The tunnel goes down and along and comes up in an old root cellar under the garden wall, where
the Academy used to keep potatoes. The girls swept it, hung a lantern, dragged in three cushions
and a trunk, and called it the **Lair**. It is headquarters. It has a trunk for treasure, a shelf
for scrolls, a board for cases, a wall for paintings, and a corner for snacks. A second tunnel
from the Lair comes up outside the wall, among the trees. That is how they get to the Woods, and
through the Woods to the town, and back before the bedtime bell.

The Headmistress does not know. Probably.

## The teachers

**The Headmistress** is tall and kind and worried. She wears her keys on a ribbon. She gives the
Pure of Heart test and the bedtime bell, and she has never, not once, looked behind the bottom
shelf.

**Professor Hoot** is the librarian, an owl with very small glasses. He teaches Myths, which means
he reads the old stories out loud and then asks tricky questions. When a princess is stuck on a
word, Professor Hoot reads it aloud, but only after she has tried once herself; he is very firm
about that. On weekends he runs the town library and gets the books mixed up.

**Madame Cinder** teaches Spells. She came from Emberfall long ago and is not afraid of anything
except a cold draft. Every spell in her class is a word, and every word is a spell. She says that
is simply how magic works.

**Mr. Abacus** is a tortoise and teaches Numbers. He is very slow and very right. He runs a
pretend market stall in the classroom and sells pretend seeds for real counting. He has never once
been hurried.

**Miss Palette** teaches Art. She has paint on her nose at all times. She discovered that Fire
Girl could burn a charcoal stick into the blackest ash paint, that Flower Girl could crush petals
into dyes, and that E could draw lines that glow in the dark, and she has not stopped talking
about it since.

**Cook** runs the cafeteria. Cook makes the ice cream. Cook is the most important person in the
school and knows it.

## The lunch friends

The cafeteria is where the older princesses sit, and a kind word at lunch is worth a great deal.

- **The Ice princess** is shy and precise and secretly loves warm things. She sits near the
  window and lines up her peas.
- **The Wind princess** never sits still, talks very fast, and knows every rumor in the school.
  She is usually the rumor.
- **The Stone princess** is quiet and steady and collects pebbles. She is the best listener in
  the school.
- **The Cloud princess** is dreamy and draws in her mashed potatoes and forgets her homework
  every single day.
- **The Shell princess** is from the seaside, giggles a lot, and brings strange snacks that
  smell of salt.
- **The Moss princess** is tiny and fierce and grows mushrooms in her desk, which is against
  several rules.

## Middlemarket and Constable Pebble

**Middlemarket** is the small town between the kingdoms where everyone shops. It has a square,
a bakery, a market, a library, an ice cream shop, and always a small problem to fix.

The big city of **Capital**, far down the river, is having its parade season, which lasts all
autumn. Capital borrowed every police officer from every town around for parade duty. That left
Middlemarket with exactly one: **Constable Pebble**. She is short and round and tremendously
determined, and she has a stack of complaints on her desk taller than she is. Missing pies. Goats
in hats. Lamps that go out. Signs that turn upside down in the night.

The first time the three princesses walked into her office, she looked at them for a long moment,
opened a drawer, and took out three badges. "Junior Helpers," she said. "Do not tell your
Headmistress."

- **Mrs. Crumb** bakes for both kingdoms. Her oven is always going out, and lately her pies are
  going missing.
- **Old Fern** grows the town's vegetables and is always losing his seeds. His goats have
  started wearing hats.
- **Pip** lights the street lamps at night and has a very short ladder.

## The troubles

Never scary. Always a bit silly.

- **Grumpy Weed** grows where it should not and complains about it.
- **Sneezy Smoke** is a puff of smoke that cannot stop sneezing soot on things. It has probably
  taken the lamps. It did not mean to.
- **Cold Snap** is a tiny frost that turns puddles to ice and makes everyone shiver.
- **Fog Puff** is a cloud that sits on a path and will not move until someone tells it a story.
- **Rumble Rock** is a boulder that rolls into the road because it is bored.

A trouble, when a princess casts a spell at it, shrinks, sneezes, sulks or wanders off. Nobody is
ever hurt. Troubles are always sorry afterwards.

## The places

1. **The Academy**: the gate, the dormitory, the hallway, four classrooms, the cafeteria, the
   library, the Headmistress's office.
2. **The tunnel and the Lair.**
3. **The Woods**: the path, the clearing, and three places that echo the old stories:
   **Persephone's Gate** (where it is spring on one side and winter on the other), the **Torch
   Cave** (where someone once hid a stolen flame), and **Thunder Peak** (the only hill tall
   enough for a Stormhaven princess to feel at home).
4. **Middlemarket**: the square, the market, Constable Pebble's office, the ice cream shop.
5. **Festival Hill**: where all the kingdoms meet once a year. This year the princesses are in
   charge.

## The myths, and why each one is theirs

Professor Hoot reads the old Greek stories, told for young listeners. Each one echoes one of the
girls, and each becomes a Myth Scroll and, later, an adventure.

| Myth | Told as | Whose story it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Persephone and the seasons** | a girl who spends half the year with her mother in the garden and half the year underground, which is why there is winter | Flower Girl, and Fire Girl's warmth that brings her back |
| **Prometheus and the gift of fire** | the one who brought fire down to people so they could cook and be warm, and got in a great deal of trouble for it | Fire Girl |
| **Demeter's garden** | the mother who made everything grow, and what happened to the fields when she was sad | Flower Girl |
| **Zeus and the thunderbolt** | the king of the sky who could never quite keep his lightning in his pocket | E |
| **Hermes the messenger** | the fastest one, with wings on his sandals, who carried every message and most of the gossip | the Wind princess, and anyone who sneaks out |
| **Athena's owl** | the wise one, and the owl who sat on her shoulder and saw everything | Professor Hoot, obviously |
| **Icarus** | the boy with wax wings who flew too high because flying was wonderful | E, who is also too much for the ground sometimes |
| **The Twelve Labors** | the strong one who had to do twelve impossible jobs, one at a time | everyone, and Mr. Abacus, who counts them |

## Rules of the world (for whoever writes new quests)

- Reading is the magic. A spell is cast by reading a word. A chest opens by building a word. A
  clue is a puzzle.
- Nobody loses anything, ever. A wrong word means "try again," never "too bad."
- Enemies are silly. Problems are small and fixable. Grown-ups in the town are kind and a little
  hopeless, so the girls get to be the clever ones.
- Fire Girl cannot help in the garden alone. Flower Girl cannot help on a cold dark night alone.
  E cannot hold still alone. Together they can do everything, and more.
- Nobody is ever sent away. The Pure of Heart test is a kindness choice, and you can always try
  again.
- Every word a player sees can be tapped to hear it.
- The Headmistress never finds the tunnel. Probably.
