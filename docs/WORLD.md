# The world of Ember Bloom

The story bible. Everything here is also in `data/world.js`, which is what the game actually
reads. Change the words there; this page is the version to read aloud at bedtime.

## The two kingdoms

**Emberfall** is a kingdom of warm stone, lanterns and bakeries. It is always a little too hot.
Its people are quick and loud and kind. Its princess is **Fire Girl**. Her magic is light and
heat: she can light a candle from across the room, warm a cold friend, and make a dark room glow.

**Petalvale** is a kingdom of meadows, orchards and bees. It is always a little too damp. Its
people are patient and clever and kind. Its princess is **Flower Girl**. Her magic is growing and
calming: she can make a seed sprout, call up a vine, and fill a square with flowers.

## Why they kept apart

Fire burns flowers. A hundred years ago, a spark from an Emberfall festival jumped the river and
burned a Petalvale orchard. Nobody was hurt, but the two kingdoms decided it was safer to keep to
their own sides of the river. Children of Emberfall were told, "Never go near the flower people,
you will hurt them." Children of Petalvale were told, "Never go near the fire people, they will
hurt you."

Both were a little bit right, and both had forgotten something.

## What they forgot

Petals hold cool dew. A vine can wrap a flame until it falls asleep. Flower Girl's gentlest spell,
**Dew** (she calls it Bloom Breath), can put out Fire Girl's fire. So fire is not simply stronger.
Neither of them can win, which is a good reason to stop trying.

And there is something better. Fire warms a seed so it sprouts early. Ash makes the soil rich. A
flower can cup a flame and hold it steady, a lantern that never burns. When the two kinds of magic
work together it is called **Ember-Bloom magic**, and nobody has seen it in a hundred years.

## The school

**Thistledown School of Small Magic** sits exactly on the river, half in each kingdom. Every child
with a little magic goes there. Seats are chosen by a hat that pulls names out of a jar, and the
hat has never once been argued with.

The hat sat Fire Girl and Flower Girl at the same desk.

On the first day, Fire Girl sneezed and set the desk on fire. Everyone ran. Flower Girl did not.
She blew gently, and her petals put the fire out. The desk was fine. Fire Girl said sorry. Flower
Girl said, "It was a very good sneeze."

They have been best friends ever since.

## The helper

**Professor Hoot** is the school librarian, an owl with very small glasses. When a player is
stuck on a word, Professor Hoot reads it aloud (but only after she has tried once herself; he is
very firm about that). On weekends he runs the town library and gets the books mixed up.

## The town

**Middlemarket** is the town between the kingdoms where everyone shops. It has a square, a bakery,
a market, a library, and always a small problem to fix.

- **Mrs. Crumb** bakes for both kingdoms. Her oven is always going out.
- **Old Fern** grows the town's vegetables and is always losing his seeds.
- **Pip** lights the street lamps at night and has a very short ladder.

## The troubles

Never scary. Always a bit silly.

- **Grumpy Weed** grows where it should not and complains about it.
- **Sneezy Smoke** is a puff of smoke that cannot stop sneezing soot on things.
- **Cold Snap** is a tiny frost that turns puddles to ice and makes everyone shiver.

## The places (in the order they open)

1. **Middlemarket**, the town square. Open from the start.
2. **Thistledown School.** Open from the start. Professor Hoot's library.
3. **Petalvale Orchard.** Apple trees, bees, and one very grumpy weed.
4. **Lantern Lane.** The Emberfall street of a hundred lamps. Tonight most are out.
5. **Frost Pond.** Between the kingdoms. It froze when it should not have. Needs both kinds of magic.
6. **Festival Hill.** Where both kingdoms meet once a year. This year the princesses are in charge.

## Rules of the world (for whoever writes new quests)

- Reading is the magic. A spell is cast by reading a word. A chest opens by building a word.
- Nobody loses anything, ever. A wrong word means "try again", never "too bad".
- Enemies are silly. Problems are small and fixable. Grown-ups in the town are kind and a little
  hopeless, so the girls get to be the clever ones.
- Fire Girl cannot help in the garden alone. Flower Girl cannot help on a cold dark night alone.
  Together they can do both, and more.
- Every word a player sees can be tapped to hear it.
